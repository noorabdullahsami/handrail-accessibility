# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29)

First public release.

- Scan the current page with axe-core 4.14.0 for WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 Level A and AA rules, including same-site frames and open shadow roots.
- The Core 8 summary and a "Fix these next" list.
- 23 guided checks in plain language. 15 of them get help from the scan, including a keyboard focus check.
- Show on page, with Copy selector and Copy HTML.
- Progress saved per page, and "Since last scan" after a rescan.
- Copy report as Markdown.
- Clear this page's data and Clear all data.
- Light and dark themes. The panel works at 320px wide and 200% text size.
