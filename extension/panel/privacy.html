Privacy: this extension

Everything happens on your computer. Nothing you scan ever leaves your browser.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any data anywhere. It makes no network requests.
  • It has no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.
  • It does not load code or settings from the internet.

What it reads

When you select "Scan this page", it reads the page you have open so it can check it. This happens in memory, in your browser. It can only read a page after you click its icon on that page.

To check whether keyboard focus can be seen, it may take a few screenshots of the page. It compares them in memory and discards them right away. They are never saved or sent anywhere.

What it saves

It saves a small amount of data in your browser, for each page you check, so your progress is still there next time:

  • The page address, without anything after a "?" or "#".
  • Your answers to the guided checks (Not checked, Looks good, Has a problem).
  • A summary of the last scan: the time, the axe-core version, and how many problems each rule found.

It does not save the content of the page.

Deleting your data

In the panel, use "Clear this page's data" or "Clear all data". Removing the extension also deletes everything it saved.
